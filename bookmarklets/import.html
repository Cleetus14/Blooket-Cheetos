  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B.
  2. Right-click the bar and choose Add page… (Edge: Add to favorites; Opera: Add to bookmarks).
  3. Name it Blooket Cheetos.
  4. Click , then paste it into the URL field and save.
